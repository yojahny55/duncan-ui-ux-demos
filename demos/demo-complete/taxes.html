Expert Tax Filing Services

At M&K Adventure, we provide comprehensive tax preparation services for both personal and business clients. Our IRS-authorized team ensures accuracy, compliance, and maximum deductions so you get the best possible refund.

Personal Tax Preparation

Whether you're a single filer, married filing jointly, or head of household, our team handles all personal tax situations including W-2 income, freelance and 1099 income, rental property income, investment gains, and more. We'll identify every deduction and credit you're entitled to.

  • Federal & State Returns (1040)
  • W-2 & 1099 Income Filing
  • Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)
  • Child Tax Credit & Dependent Claims
  • Itemized & Standard Deductions
  • ITIN Applications & Renewals

Business Tax Preparation

From sole proprietors to LLCs and corporations, we handle the full spectrum of business tax filings. Our team understands the complexities of business deductions, quarterly estimates, payroll taxes, and multi-state filing requirements to keep your business compliant and your tax burden minimized.

  • LLC & Corporation Returns (1120, 1120-S, 1065)
  • Schedule C for Sole Proprietors
  • Quarterly Estimated Tax Planning
  • Payroll Tax Filings (940, 941)
  • Business Expense Deductions
  • Sales Tax & Annual Report Filings

Tax Preparation Checklist

Gather these documents before your appointment to ensure a smooth and efficient tax filing experience.

W-2 Forms from all employers
1099 Forms (freelance, interest, dividends)
Social Security cards for you and dependents
Valid photo ID (driver's license or passport)
Bank account & routing numbers (for direct deposit)
Previous year's tax return (if available)
Mortgage interest statement (Form 1098)
Childcare provider information & expenses
Health insurance statements (Form 1095)
Records of charitable donations
Student loan interest statement (Form 1098-E)
Business income & expense records (if self-employed)

The standard IRS deadline for filing personal tax returns is April 15th each year. If you need more time, we can help you file an extension (Form 4868) which gives you until October 15th. However, any taxes owed are still due by April 15th to avoid penalties and interest.
