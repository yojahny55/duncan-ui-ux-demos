Shipping to Cuba

Reliable, affordable, and fast package delivery from Tampa to anywhere in Cuba. We handle your shipments with care so your loved ones receive exactly what they need.

Ship a Package Ask on WhatsApp

Send Packages to Cuba with Confidence

At M&K Adventure, we understand how important it is to stay connected with your family in Cuba. Our shipping service provides a safe, reliable, and affordable way to send packages from Tampa, FL directly to your loved ones anywhere on the island.

We handle everything from packing assistance to customs documentation, ensuring your shipment arrives safely and on time. With years of experience shipping to Cuba, we know the best routes and procedures to get your packages delivered efficiently.

What You Can Ship

  • Clothing, shoes & personal items
  • Food & non-perishable goods
  • Medicine & hygiene products
  • Electronics & appliances
  • Household supplies & cleaning products
  • Baby items & toys

How It Works

Ship in 3 Easy Steps

1

Pack Your Items

Gather the items you want to send. We can help you with packing materials and advice on what can be shipped to Cuba.

2

Bring your package to our Tampa office or schedule a convenient pickup. We'll weigh it, process the paperwork, and handle customs documentation.

3

Your package is shipped safely to Cuba and delivered directly to your family's door. We provide tracking updates so you always know where your shipment is.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Delivery times depend on the shipping plan you choose. Our Basic plan takes 15-21 business days, Standard takes 10-14 business days, and Express takes 7-10 business days. Times may vary depending on the destination province and customs processing.

Prohibited items include firearms, explosives, illegal substances, perishable foods, live animals, and certain chemicals. There are also restrictions on the quantity of electronics per shipment. Contact us for a complete list of restricted items before packing your shipment.

There is no strict weight limit per shipment, but individual boxes should not exceed 70 pounds for safe handling. If you have a large shipment, we can split it into multiple boxes. Pricing is calculated per pound, so you only pay for what you ship.

Yes! All our shipping plans include tracking. Basic plans receive periodic email updates, Standard plans include real-time tracking through our system, and Express plans add SMS alerts at every stage of delivery. You can also call us anytime for a status update.

Yes, we offer pickup services within the Tampa Bay area for an additional fee. You can also drop off your packages at our Tampa office during business hours. For large or heavy shipments, we recommend scheduling a pickup for your convenience.
