FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

An LLC (Limited Liability Company) offers flexible management, pass-through taxation, and fewer compliance requirements. A Corporation is better suited for businesses planning to raise investment capital through stock issuance, but requires a Board of Directors, annual meetings, and more formal record-keeping.
Standard processing with the State of Florida typically takes 5-10 business days. With our Premium and Corporation packages, we offer priority processing that can reduce this to 3-5 business days. Expedited 24-hour filing is also available for an additional fee.
Yes, Florida law requires every LLC and Corporation to have a registered agent with a physical address in the state. A registered agent receives legal documents and official correspondence on behalf of your business. All our packages include registered agent service for the first year.
Yes! Florida allows non-US residents and foreign nationals to form an LLC or Corporation. You do not need to be a US citizen or permanent resident. We can assist with the entire process, including obtaining your EIN without a Social Security Number by using an ITIN or other accepted identification.
Florida LLCs and Corporations must file an Annual Report with the Division of Corporations each year (due by May 1st). LLCs pay a $138.75 fee and Corporations pay a $150 fee. Florida has no state income tax, which is one of the major advantages of forming your business here. Our Premium package includes a compliance calendar to help you stay on track.
