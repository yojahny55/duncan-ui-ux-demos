About M&K Adventure

Tampa's trusted multiservice hub — your one-stop destination for travel, shipping, tax preparation, notary services, and business formation.

5+ Years of Service

Our Story

M&K Adventure was founded in Tampa, Florida, with a clear mission: to provide the Hispanic community and the broader U.S. market with reliable, professional, and accessible multiservice solutions. What started as a small travel agency quickly grew into something much bigger — a full-service hub where families, entrepreneurs, and individuals could find everything they needed under one roof.

Over the years, we expanded our services to include shipping to Cuba, tax preparation, notary services, and business formation. Each service was added in direct response to what our community asked for — because at M&K Adventure, we listen first and act second.

Today, M&K Adventure stands as Tampa's premier multiservice business, proudly serving clients both locally and across the United States. Our bilingual team works tirelessly to ensure every client receives personalized, high-quality service — whether you're booking a dream vacation, starting a new business, or sending a package to loved ones in Cuba.
