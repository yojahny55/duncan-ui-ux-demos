Comprehensive Notary Services

At M&K Adventure, our certified notary public provides reliable and professional notarization services for all types of legal documents. Whether you need a quick signature notarization or a complex real estate closing, we are here to help ensure your documents are properly executed and legally valid.

Documents We Notarize

  • Affidavits and sworn statements
  • Powers of Attorney (POA)
  • Real estate documents and deeds
  • Loan documents and mortgage closings
  • Wills, trusts, and estate planning documents
  • Business contracts and agreements
  • Immigration and travel consent forms
  • Vehicle title transfers

All notarizations are performed in compliance with Florida state law. Our bilingual team (English & Spanish) ensures clear communication throughout the process.

Mobile Notary Service

Can't make it to our office? No problem. Our mobile notary service brings professional notarization directly to your home, office, hospital, or any convenient location throughout the Tampa Bay area. We offer flexible scheduling including evenings and weekends to accommodate your busy lifestyle. Perfect for real estate closings, loan signings, elderly or homebound clients, and business professionals.

How It Works

Three Simple Steps

1

Schedule Appointment

Call, WhatsApp, or use our online form to book a time that works for you. Walk-ins are also welcome during business hours.

2

Bring Valid ID & Documents

Bring a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID) along with the documents you need notarized. Do not sign beforehand.

3

Get Your Documents Notarized

Our notary will verify your identity, witness your signature, and apply the official notary seal. Your documents are now legally notarized and ready to use.
