SIGNAL DISTORTION

We harness the raw aesthetic of chromatic aberration—the beautiful color fringing that occurs when light bends through imperfect lenses. This visual language speaks to a generation raised on VHS tapes and CRT monitors.

Our designs channel the energy of retro tech failures, transforming glitches and errors into intentional art. Every RGB split tells a story of analog warmth in a digital world.
