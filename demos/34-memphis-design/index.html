About Us

The Memphis Manifesto

Born in 1981 Milan, Memphis Design rejected minimalism and embraced bold colors, asymmetric shapes, and playful patterns.

Geometric Rebellion

Triangles, circles, squiggles and zigzags combine in unexpected ways, creating visual tension and delight.

Color Explosion

Hot pink meets teal, yellow clashes with purple. Memphis colors don't match—they CLASH beautifully.

Pattern Play

Dots, stripes, and abstract patterns layer endlessly, creating depth and visual energy everywhere.

Joyful Chaos

Design doesn't have to be serious. Memphis proves that fun and function can coexist perfectly.
