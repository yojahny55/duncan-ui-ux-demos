Why Accessibility Matters

15% of the world's population experiences some form of disability. Inclusive design isn't just ethical—it's essential.

Visual Accessibility

High contrast ratios, scalable text, and screen reader compatibility ensure content is perceivable by all users.

Keyboard Navigation

Every interactive element is accessible via keyboard, with clear focus indicators and logical tab order.

Cognitive Support

Clear layouts, consistent navigation, and simple language help users with cognitive differences.

Hearing Accessibility

Captions, transcripts, and visual alternatives for audio content ensure deaf users aren't excluded.
